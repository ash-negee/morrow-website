Data Deletion

Effective date: September 25, 2026

Morrow provides users with a way to request deletion of personal information associated with their Morrow account.

How to Request Deletion

To request deletion of your Morrow account and associated personal data, contact us using the email address below.

Email: YOUR-PRIVACY-EMAIL@example.com

Please include the email address associated with your Morrow account and, if applicable, the Instagram account associated with the Morrow integration.

What May Be Deleted

Depending on your request and applicable legal requirements, deletion may include:

  • Morrow account information.
  • Instagram account connection information.
  • Saved items.
  • User-provided capture messages and notes.
  • Saved-item titles and associated metadata.
  • Scheduling and reminder information.
  • Other personal information associated with your account.

Information We May Need to Retain

Some information may need to be retained where required by law, necessary to resolve disputes, prevent fraud or abuse, maintain security, or satisfy legitimate legal obligations.

Where information is retained for these reasons, it will be handled in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

Processing Requests

We may need to verify your identity or account ownership before processing a deletion request.

We will process valid deletion requests within a reasonable period and in accordance with applicable law.

Instagram Connection

Disconnecting your Instagram account from Morrow may stop Morrow from receiving new information through the integration, but it does not necessarily delete information that was previously stored.

If you want previously stored Morrow information deleted, submit a deletion request using the contact information above.

Contact

For questions about data deletion, contact:

Email: YOUR-PRIVACY-EMAIL@example.com
