Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 25, 2026

Morrow ("Morrow", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and the choices available to you when you use Morrow.

Morrow is designed to help you save content you discover online, organize those saved items, and remember to take action on them later.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

When you create or use a Morrow account, we may collect information such as:

  • Name or display name.
  • Email address.
  • Account identifiers.
  • Time zone and notification preferences.

1.2 Instagram Information

If you connect an Instagram professional account or interact with Morrow through Instagram, we may receive information made available to Morrow through Meta's applicable APIs and permissions.

This may include:

  • Instagram account identifiers.
  • Instagram username or other publicly available profile information.
  • Messages sent to Morrow.
  • Information about content shared with Morrow.
  • URLs associated with shared Instagram content.

We only request and process Instagram information that is relevant to providing Morrow's functionality and that is made available to us through the applicable Meta platform permissions.

1.3 Saved Content

When you save content to Morrow, we may store information associated with that saved item, including:

  • The source URL.
  • The source content or media identifier, where available.
  • The source creator's username, where available.
  • A message or note that you provide when saving the item.
  • A title associated with the saved item.
  • AI-generated summaries or organization information.
  • Your selected or inferred intention for the item.
  • Scheduling and reminder information.
  • Completion, archive, and status information.
  • Notes that you add to the saved item.

1.4 Automatically Processed Information

Morrow may use automated systems, including artificial intelligence, to help organize saved content. Depending on the feature you use, automated processing may generate information such as:

  • A fallback title.
  • A summary.
  • A category.
  • An estimated time required for an activity.
  • A suggested action.
  • An interpretation of the reason an item was saved.

If you provide an explicit message or intention when saving an item, Morrow may use that information as the primary user-provided context for the saved item.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Create and maintain your Morrow account.
  • Connect your supported Instagram account to Morrow.
  • Receive and store content that you intentionally send to Morrow.
  • Organize and display your saved items.
  • Generate summaries and other organization information.
  • Schedule reminders and notifications.
  • Allow you to mark saved items as completed or archived.
  • Provide search and retrieval functionality.
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the service.
  • Diagnose technical problems and improve the service.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Instagram and Meta

Morrow uses services and APIs provided by Meta and Instagram to support applicable Instagram integrations.

Your use of Instagram remains subject to Instagram's own terms, policies, and privacy practices. Morrow does not control how Instagram operates its platform.

Morrow only accesses information made available through the permissions and APIs applicable to the integration.

4. Content Shared With Morrow

When you intentionally send content to Morrow, such as an Instagram post or Reel, Morrow may store the URL and associated metadata needed to provide the save-and-reminder functionality.

Morrow is not intended to download and permanently re-host Instagram videos or other third-party media. Where applicable, saved content may link back to the original platform.

5. AI and Automated Processing

Morrow may use third-party AI or machine-learning services to process information associated with saved items for features such as summarization, categorization, and suggested actions.

We aim to send only the information reasonably necessary to provide the relevant feature.

AI-generated information may be incomplete or inaccurate and should be treated as assistance rather than authoritative advice.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information with service providers that help us operate Morrow, such as infrastructure, database, notification, analytics, security, and AI service providers.

Service providers receive information only as reasonably necessary to provide their services to Morrow and are expected to handle that information appropriately.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect the rights or security of Morrow or its users, or in connection with a business transaction such as a merger, acquisition, or asset sale.

7. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain your account, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

You may request deletion of your Morrow data as described in our Data Deletion page.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security, and you should understand that transmission of information over the internet carries inherent risks.

9. Your Choices

Depending on the features available to you, you may be able to:

  • Edit saved-item information.
  • Archive saved items.
  • Delete your saved information.
  • Disconnect supported integrations.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Manage notification preferences.

10. Children's Privacy

Morrow is not intended for children under the age at which they can lawfully use the service without parental consent in their jurisdiction.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

11. Third-Party Services

Morrow may rely on third-party services for infrastructure, authentication, analytics, AI processing, notifications, and integrations.

Those services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as Morrow's features, integrations, or legal requirements change.

When we make material changes, we may update the effective date shown at the top of this page and provide additional notice where required.
