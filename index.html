What is Morrow?

Morrow is a personal save-and-action tool designed to help you turn interesting discoveries into things you can revisit and act on.

You can save content, add your own context or intention, and organize saved items for later. Morrow may also use automated processing to help summarize and organize saved content.

Capture

Save interesting content when you discover it.

Remember

Keep saved items available for a later time.

Act

Turn saved ideas into actions instead of forgotten bookmarks.
